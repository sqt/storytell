## What is it?
Everyone has stories worth sharing... and everyone appreciates an engaging story! But how often are we able to take the time and really listen to the stories that other people have to share?
At *Storytell* we'll carve out a bit of time and space to hear some true stories from other people's lives. And we'll do it with drinks, snacks, and a bit of socialising!

## Questions & Answers

### What kind of stories should I expect?
That's mostly up to each storyteller, but expect each story to be based on true events from their own life and last approximately 10 minutes. For more information, see the section on "What's expected of a storyteller?"

### Will there be a common theme to the stories?
Yes. Each evening has a theme, and the stories will be loosely tied to it. See [Next Event]({{ "/" | relative_url }}) for the current theme.

### How many stories will there be?
We hope to have six storytellers, giving us about an hour's worth of stories in total. We'll have an interval in the middle for snacks & chat.

### Is there an age limit?
Yes. The evening is for ages 13 and over, as some stories may explore mature themes.

### Can I be a storyteller?
Absolutely! Anyone can tell a story. Send us a short message about your story idea by text or WhatsApp. We'll meet with each storyteller before the evening to explore their idea and help shape it for Storytell. After that, we can offer more feedback as needed. See [Tell a Story]({{ "/storytellers/" | relative_url }}) for more information.

### What's expected of a storyteller?
Storytellers are expected to stand up in front of a bunch of folk (friends and maybe even strangers!) and tell a story based on true events from their own life. The story should last about 10 minutes and be practised enough to tell without notes. See [Tell a Story]({{ "/storytellers/" | relative_url }}) for more information on telling stories.

### What's expected of a listener?
An attitude of generosity and curiosity. :-)
